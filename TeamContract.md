## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* Use Instagram and Discord as main communication medium

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages 1 day

* Teammate should notify if they are unable to make it or will be late to lectures, tutorials, tests or meetings

* Respectful and inclusive behaviour are necessary for smooth and productive communication. Each teammate should actively listen to one another and contribute any ideas
* In meetings, every teammate should try their best to focus on working on the task.

## Decision Making

* Decisions should be made by majority vote. 
  * Give everyone a chance to input their reasons and ideas before voting. Don't shut down each other's ideas.

---
## Conflict resolution

* Listen to both perspectives and decide by voting. Don't make it personal.

---

## Accountability

* Every teammate should complete the task they are assigned. If they have any issues or need help, they should notify the team. Team members should help each other understand tasks or when encountering a problem that no one knows how to solve, collectively reach out to TA/professor.
